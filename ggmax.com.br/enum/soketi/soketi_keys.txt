## Attack Surface for webapp
- **Subscription injection**: subscribe to public channels that may broadcast sensitive data (product/order update channels). Need to discover channel naming scheme.
- **JWT forgery**: if webapp can forge JWT as another user (e.g., admin or victim), can get Soketi auth for that user → receive their realtime events (orders, notifications, PII).
- **Public channel enumeration**: test common channel names: `products`, `orders`, `notifications`, `chat`, etc.
- The app key itself is public — main risk is what data flows over public/ presence channels.
