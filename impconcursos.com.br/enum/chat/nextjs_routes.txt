## IDOR assessment
- publicTypebot IDs and typebotIds are Typebot-generated high-entropy slugs (random, non-sequential). **Enumeration/IDOR by low-ID guessing is NOT viable.**
- Builder API (`/api/typebots`) properly requires auth (401).
- File-upload presigned-URL endpoint is not auth-gated but requires a valid typebotId (non-enumerable) — by design for public chat conversations. Not an exploitable IDOR without a leaked typebotId.
