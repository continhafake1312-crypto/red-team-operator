FINDING F-009: Authenticated Information Disclosure — Full Commercial Catalog & Tenant Company/Tax Data via /portal/getProdutos
==========================================================================================================================
Hosts: www.wcursos.com.br (PROD) — confirmed; same endpoint exists on TESTE/WEAD (TESTE requires its own session)
Severity: MEDIUM (CVSS est. 4.3 — AV:N/AC:L/PR:L/UI:N/S:U/C:L/I:N/A:N; authenticated, low-sensitivity but broad commercial + tax data)
Date: 2026-08-27 (Fase 7 — discovered during admin-escalation probing)

## Summary
Any authenticated portal user (including a low-privilege, course-less STUDENT
account — confirmed with the F-003 takeover account "SILVANA") can retrieve the
merchant's COMPLETE product/service catalog together with each product's
multi-tenant company record (legal name, CNPJ) and tax-classification codes.
This is commercial/financial data not presented to students in the normal UI
and discloses the platform's internal multi-tenant structure, full pricing,
and tax configuration.

## Vulnerable Endpoint
`GET /portal/getProdutos?format=json` (authenticated; no role check)

## Reproduction (non-destructive, read-only)

### Step 1 — Unauthenticated request is correctly blocked:
  curl --proxy socks5://127.0.0.1:9050 -sk \
    "https://www.wcursos.com.br/portal/getProdutos?format=json"
  -> HTTP 302  Location: /portal/login       (auth is enforced)

### Step 2 — Authenticated student session dumps the whole catalog:
  curl --proxy socks5://127.0.0.1:9050 -sk -b cookies_post.txt \
    "https://www.wcursos.com.br/portal/getProdutos?format=json" -o gpr.json
  -> HTTP 200, 344088 bytes, JSON array of 52 product objects.

## Impact
- Discloses the merchant's complete commercial strategy: full pricing table,
  discount ladders (à vista/boleto/PIX), installment plans, launch/closure
  dates, sale quantities, and stock/matricula caps — competitive intelligence.
- Discloses internal multi-tenant structure and the LEGAL/TAX identity of each
  tenant (company legal name + CNPJ for the PROD and WEAD companies), plus two
  internal TEST tenant companies ("Empresa de Testes", "WRM2") — confirming a
  vendor-side multi-tenant SaaS where each tenant's company data is reachable
  by any student of any tenant.
- Tax-classification data (CFOP, NCM, ICMS/IPI/PIS/COFINS CST + aliquots, NF
  type) is exposed — useful for fraud/tax-evasion profiling and confirms the
  backend is a full e-commerce (com.rlg.ecommerce) instance, not just an LMS.
- A student account that has purchased NOTHING and is enrolled in NOTHING
  (SILVANA) retrieves the entire catalog — i.e. the endpoint is not scoped to
  products the student owns; it returns the whole merchant catalog.

## Reproduction files
- webapp/gpr.json — full catalog dump (52 products). Contains real CNPJ/
  pricing data; sanitize before external sharing.

## Recommendation
1. Scope /portal/getProdutos (and similar catalog endpoints) to the caller's
   role: students should only see the storefront listing they are entitled to,
   never the full administrative catalog with tax codes, company CNPJ, sale
   quantities, and discount ladders.
2. Strip empresaTO (CNPJ, razao social) and tax fields (CFOP/NCM/CST/aliquots)
   from any student-facing catalog response.
3. Apply role-based authorization (a filter, not just in-controller checks)
   to catalog/admin endpoints — see the access-control design note in F-008.
4. Consider whether /portal/getProdutos should exist at all on the student
   portal (it appears to be a backoffice/catalog-management endpoint exposed
   on the student surface).
