## Ownership analysis (why this is NOT the alvo's)
  1. Content semantics: "bilhete" = raffle TICKET, "valor" = PRIZE value,
     "concurso" = DRAW number. This is a lottery/raffle results dataset.
  2. The alvo (centraldeconcursos.com.br) is a CIVIL-SERVICE EXAM PREP
     company. "Concurso" in the alvo's context = competitive public exam
     posting; the alvo does NOT run lotteries or sell raffle tickets.
     The fields {bilhete, valor} have no meaning in an exam-prep domain.
  3. The alvo's web presence (50.418 wayback URLs, 1.958 JS files) contains
     ZERO references to storage.googleapis.com, blob.core.windows.net, or
     s3.amazonaws.com. The alvo does not consume this bucket.
  4. The bucket name "concursos" is generic and shared by many Brazilian
     lottery/exam entities; collision is coincidental.

## Risk to the alvo: NONE.
## Risk to the third party: an unauthorized party can enumerate and download
   the full lottery results dataset (230 MB). No PII observed in sampled
   objects (only ticket numbers + prize values), but full review not done.
   This is the third party's responsibility, out of this engagement's scope.

## Recommendation for the engagement

  - Do NOT attempt write/PUT (would be attacking a non-target asset).
