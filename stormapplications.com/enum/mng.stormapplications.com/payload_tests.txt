## SQL Injection - /api/login (POST)
All returned 403 FORBIDDEN:
- `admin' OR '1'='1`
- `admin" OR "1"="1`
- `admin' OR 1=1--`
- `admin' UNION SELECT 1,2,3--`
- `admin'/*`
- `admin'--`

## NoSQL Injection - /api/login (POST)
All returned 403 FORBIDDEN:
- `{"username":"admin","password":{"$ne":""}}`
- `username[$ne]=admin&password[$ne]=x`
