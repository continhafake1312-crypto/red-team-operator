    https://focusconcursos.com.br/_next/image?q=100&url=https%3A%2F%2Fs3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com%2Farquivos.grupofocus.com.br%2Fadmin%2F4%2Fteachers%2Fcm7ncz9g10heejxqn33mw9htq.webp

Implication (exploitable leak - passie phase): Next.js covers up S3 objects on the
   `arquivos.grupofocus.com.br` bucket through `/_next/image` — objects are
   publicly reachable on S3 but the GET for their direct S3 URLs is blocked by admin ACLs
   on the bucket level, pointing to a same-bucket proxy path with limited object list.
Attack surface consequence: any front-end or admin hub that consumes a _next/image
   URL with a mapped webp object-key may allow image-path enumeration that leads to
   Admin panel management URLs (PDF's thumbnails, teacher profiles, product images,
   account-staging assets).
Used in evidence P-004/008.
