======================================================================
F-002: Email Spoofing — SPF Softfail + DMARC p=none
Severity: MEDIUM (🟡)
Date: 2026-09-13
======================================================================

DESCRIPTION:
The domain cfcdigitaloficial.com.br has a weak email security policy:
- SPF: ~all (softfail — allows unauthorized senders)
- DMARC policy: p=none (no enforcement)
This allows attackers to send spoofed emails appearing to be from
@cfcdigitaloficial.com.br addresses.

AFFECTED:
- @cfcdigitaloficial.com.br emails (administrative/financial domain)

EVIDENCE:
$ dig +short TXT cfcdigitaloficial.com.br
"v=spf1 include:api-mail.com ~all"

$ dig +short _dmarc.cfcdigitaloficial.com.br TXT
(No DMARC record — policy defaults to p=none)

IMPACT:
- Phishing emails from "contato@cfcdigitaloficial.com.br",
  "admin@cfcdigitaloficial.com.br", "financeiro@cfcdigitaloficial.com.br"
  would pass SPF checks against receiving servers
- Very likely to reach clients' inboxes (no DMARC quarantine/reject)
- Reputation damage for CFC Digital brand

RECOMMENDATION:
1. Change SPF to -all (hardfail)
2. Create DMARC record: "v=DMARC1; p=quarantine; rua=mailto:..."
3. Implement DKIM signing for outgoing emails
