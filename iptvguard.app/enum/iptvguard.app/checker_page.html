Troubleshooting

Common Failure Causes

Understanding why an analysis might fail

Expired Subscription

Your IPTV subscription may have ended or been deactivated. Contact your provider to check your account status and renew if needed.

Incorrect URL

A typo, missing parameter, or wrong format in your playlist URL can cause the analysis to fail. Make sure to copy the full link provided by your provider.

Server Offline

Your provider's server may be temporarily down for maintenance or experiencing issues. Try again later or check with your provider.

Wrong Credentials

Usernames and passwords are case-sensitive. Make sure you're entering them exactly as provided. Your provider may also have changed them.
